Start here

  1. Plug in the charger, set brightness to full and volume to about half.
  2. Colours (1 min): tap through each screen, hold to exit.
  3. Touch (1 min): paint the whole grid green, then 5 or more fingers at once.
  4. Speakers (30 s): landscape, Left, Right, Sweep, then Loud.
  5. Mic (15 s): record 5 seconds and play it back.
  6. Cameras (1 min): Front, then Back, take a snapshot of each.
  7. Motion and Pencil (30 s each, Pencil only if you have one).

Screen colours

Full brightness first. Tap to move through black, white, red, green, blue, grey and a gradient. Look for dots that stay one colour (dead or stuck pixels), bright edges on black (backlight bleed), and blotches or tint on white and grey.

Exit: tap the Exit button (it shows for a moment after each tap), press and hold for 1 second, or tap with two fingers.

Touch grid and multi-touch

Opens a full screen grid. Drag a finger over every cell until it all turns green. Then put up to 10 fingers down at once: the counter should follow each finger.

Exit with the Done button in the corner. Tap Move to shift that bar so you can reach the cells under it.

Stereo speakers

Hold the iPad in landscape at medium volume. Left should come from the left end, right from the right end, and the sweep should glide across. The bass tone should not buzz or rattle.

Microphone

Allow microphone access, speak for 5 seconds from arm's length, then play it back. Listen for muffled or crackly sound.

Front and back cameras

Check both cameras show a sharp, clean picture. Point the back camera at text up close and far away and look for blur patches, dust spots or haze.

Motion sensors

Tap Start and allow Motion and Orientation Access. Tilt the iPad slowly: the bubble and numbers should move smoothly. Flat on a table, tilt should read close to 0.

Apple Pencil and drawing pad

Draw across the pad with a finger and, if you have one, an Apple Pencil (USB-C or 1st generation). Lines should follow the tip with no gaps. With a Pencil, rest your palm on the pad: it should not draw.

Screen info

At default display settings an iPad (A16) reports 820 x 1180 points (either order) with a pixel ratio of 2, matching its 2360 by 1640 pixel display. Max touch points above 1 means multi-touch is reported.

Fast tricks in Settings and apps

  1. Battery in seconds. Pull down on the Home Screen, type battery, open Battery Health. Note maximum capacity, cycle count and first use date.
  2. Serial and warranty. Settings > General > About, press and hold Serial Number to copy it, then check it at checkcoverage.apple.com.
  3. Repair history. Settings > General > About. A Parts and Service History section appears only if a part was replaced. Unknown next to a part means non-genuine or unverified.
  4. Company or school lock (MDM). Settings > General > VPN & Device Management. There should be no management profile.
  5. Microphone. Voice Memos: record 10 seconds and play it back.
  6. Both cameras. Camera app: take a photo and a short video with the rear and the front camera, then flip through them in Photos.
  7. Apple Pencil. Notes > new note > drawing tool. Draw across the whole screen and check for gaps.
  8. Close look at marks. Magnifier app on your own phone helps you inspect scratches, the port and the camera glass.
  9. Erase in front of you. Settings > General > Transfer or Reset iPad > Erase All Content and Settings. The seller enters their Apple Account password if asked.
  10. Confirm no Activation Lock. After the erase, go through setup. It must not ask for the previous owner's Apple Account and must not show a Remote Management screen.
